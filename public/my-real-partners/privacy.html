My Real Partners

Privacy policy

My Real Partners is operated by SM SOLUTIONS MARKETING. It lets eligible real estate partners view the clients, properties, booking progress, installment plans and payment records associated with their partner access.

Information we use

We use your registered mobile number and app password to verify access. At first sign-in, your registered Aadhaar number serves as the initial credential, and the Bookings service creates an app account linked to the existing eligible partner record. A password you choose through a password change or reset replaces that initial credential. Password recovery uses your registered mobile number and Aadhaar to verify the request and sends a code to the email address already registered with the service.

The app displays your partner profile, associated client contact information, linked properties, booking milestones, installment schedules, and payment and refund records supplied by the Bookings service. Client contact information can include name, mobile number, email and postal address. The partner app does not display client Aadhaar, PAN, bank details or identity documents. It shows recorded payment information and does not initiate or process payments.

The app includes Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which can initialize when the app starts, before sign-in or permission to display notifications. Firebase may process installation and technical identifiers at that time. When notifications are set up for your signed-in account, the service associates a notification token and device platform with your app account to deliver installment reminders. Notifications use generic reminder text; client names, property references and payment amounts are loaded inside the authenticated app. The app can also schedule reminders on your device.

Our service and its hosting providers process technical information needed to operate and protect the service, including network information and request logs. If you contact support, we use the information you provide to respond to your request.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate partners, enforce access to associated records, display the partner workspace, provide password recovery, send enabled reminders, protect the service and respond to support and privacy requests.

Service providers

The app communicates with the Bookings service, hosted on Render, for authentication and account records. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers remote notifications. These providers process information needed to operate their services. Read Google's Firebase privacy information for more about Firebase.

Device storage and security

The app communicates with the service over HTTPS. On Android, the app stores the signed-in session in encrypted device storage. Passwords are not saved by the app. Financial and client records are held in app memory rather than saved as an offline account database. The app also stores device preferences and information needed for enabled local reminders.

A session can last up to 30 days. Signing out, session expiry or server revocation clears the saved session. Signing out attempts to unregister the device's notification token and clear local reminders. These actions do not delete the app account or the underlying business records.

Retention and account requests

We retain information as needed to operate the app, maintain booking and transaction records, protect the service and handle requests. The underlying partner, client, property, booking and payment records may need to be retained independently of app access. Contact us for information about retention of a particular record.

You can request closure of your My Real Partners app account and deletion of associated app login and notification information by emailing support@myrealassets.in. We verify requests through registered contact details and explain what can be removed, any records that need to be retained and the outcome. Read the account and data deletion request instructions.

Firebase holds installation identifiers separately from the notification tokens kept by Bookings. Removing a Bookings token does not itself delete the Firebase installation identifier or associated Firebase data. Contact support about handling this data as part of your request.

Contact

For support, privacy questions or account and data requests, contact SM SOLUTIONS MARKETING at support@myrealassets.in. Do not email passwords, Aadhaar numbers, identity documents, client details or payment details.
