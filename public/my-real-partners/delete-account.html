My Real Partners

Account and data deletion requests

My Real Partners is operated by SM SOLUTIONS MARKETING. You can request closure of your app account and deletion of associated app login and notification information. You can also ask about deletion of specific app data.

How to make a request

Email support@myrealassets.in from your registered email address with the subject “My Real Partners account deletion request”. State whether you want to close the app account or request deletion of specific app data.

We review requests and verify account ownership through the contact details already registered with the service. If you cannot use your registered email, contact the same support address for help with verification. Do not send your password, Aadhaar number, identity documents, client information or payment details by email.

What your request can cover

Your request can cover closure of My Real Partners app access and removal of app login information, registered notification devices and associated app notification data. Support will explain the action taken and any information that must be retained. This is a support-assisted process; sending an email does not instantly delete an account.

Underlying partner, client, property, booking and payment records may need to be retained independently of app access. A request to close an app account does not itself cancel a booking, change financial obligations or delete another person's records. We will explain how your request affects the records involved.

Firebase holds installation identifiers separately from Bookings notification tokens. Removing a server token does not itself delete the Firebase identifier or associated Firebase data. Ask support about handling this data as part of your request. Read Google's Firebase privacy information.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not close your app account. You can submit a request using the support address above even if you have uninstalled the app.
