Account deletion requests

My Real Assets is operated by SM Solutions Marketing. You can request deletion of your My Real Assets app account and associated app data.

Email support@myrealassets.in with the subject “My Real Assets account deletion request”.

Our office will review your request and may need to verify that you are the account owner before completing it. Do not include your password, Aadhaar number, identity documents or payment details in your email.

What happens to your information

Your request can cover your app account, login and notification information. Firebase holds installation identifiers separately from the notification tokens kept by Bookings; removing a server notification token does not itself delete the Firebase installation identifier or associated Firebase data. Our office can explain how this data is handled when responding. See Google’s Firebase privacy information. Bookings retains property and payment records indefinitely so the office can maintain its property and transaction history. Our office can explain how your request affects your records when responding.

You can also find the deletion request option in the app’s Account section.
