MY REAL ASSETS

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

SM SOLUTIONS MARKETING operates My Real Assets. The app lets eligible customers view their linked property, installment, payment and refund records.

Information we use

We use your registered mobile number and password to authenticate you. We use customer and identity records held by our office, including Aadhaar information in the Bookings system, to identify and link eligible accounts. The app displays your name, account identifier, linked property information and financial records. It does not initiate or process payments.

To provide notifications, our service associates a Firebase Cloud Messaging token and device platform with an account. Google Firebase also processes installation-related identifiers and technical details such as app version. Our hosting and service providers may process network and device information needed to operate and secure their services.

How we use and share information

We use information to authenticate users, provide property records, protect the service, respond to support requests and send notifications. We use the Bookings service hosted on Render and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging as service providers. See Firebase privacy information for details of Google's processing.

Security and retention

Connections use HTTPS. The Android app stores its session using encrypted device storage. A session normally expires after up to one day; signing out attempts to unregister the notification token.

We intend to retain customer, property, payment and app login records indefinitely to maintain our business records. The service removes some notification tokens and deletes notification events older than 30 days. Logs, backups and Firebase data may have different retention periods. Contact us for information about a specific record.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact SM SOLUTIONS MARKETING at support@myrealassets.in. Do not include passwords, Aadhaar numbers, identity documents or payment details in your email.
