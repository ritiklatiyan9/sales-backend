My Real Assets

Privacy policy

My Real Assets is operated by SM SOLUTIONS MARKETING. This policy describes the information used by the My Real Assets mobile app and the choices available to you.

Information we use

The app uses your registered mobile number and password to sign you in. App access must first be issued by the office. Depending on the account setup, you use an office invitation to choose a password or complete the password-change step for an office-provisioned account. The Bookings service uses your existing verified customer identity record, including Aadhaar information, to link and check eligibility for the app account; you do not need to send Aadhaar details to the app during setup. Your name and account identifier, linked property details, installment schedules, and payment and refund records are provided by the Bookings service and displayed in the app. The app lets you view records; it does not initiate or process payments.

If you enable notifications, the service associates a Firebase Cloud Messaging token and device platform with your account to deliver property activity updates. Firebase also processes installation-related identifiers, Firebase user agent and app version. Firebase installation identifiers are held by Firebase separately from notification tokens. Removing a notification token from the Bookings service does not itself delete the Firebase installation identifier or the data Firebase associates with it. Contact our office for information about how this data is handled. Service providers may receive technical information needed to operate their services, such as network and device information.

Why we use it

We use this information to verify access to your account, show your property and payment records, operate and protect the service, respond to support requests and send notifications when enabled.

Service providers

We use the Bookings service, hosted on Render, to provide app data and authentication. We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications. These service providers process information as needed to provide their services. You can learn about Firebase’s data practices in Google’s Firebase privacy information.

Security and retention

The app communicates with the Bookings service over HTTPS. The service stores passwords and invitation codes as hashes. On Android, the app stores its session using encrypted device storage. Sessions expire after up to one day, and signing out attempts to unregister the notification token. Bookings retains property and payment records indefinitely so the office can maintain its property and transaction history. Other information is retained as needed to operate the app and handle requests. Contact us for information about the retention of a specific record.

Account and data requests

You can request deletion of your My Real Assets app account and associated app data by contacting us. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Bookings retains property and payment records indefinitely so the office can maintain its property and transaction history. Read the account deletion request page for instructions.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact SM SOLUTIONS MARKETING at support@myrealassets.in. Do not send passwords, Aadhaar numbers, identity documents or payment details by email.
